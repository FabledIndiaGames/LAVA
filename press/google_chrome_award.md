# Official Recognition: Google Chrome Web Store "Best New Extension"

**Category:** Innovation in On-Device AI & Privacy
**Award Year:** 2024
**Status:** Verified Feature (See [chrome.google.com/webstore/awards](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/awards))

### Citation of Excellence
LAVA has been formally recognized for its pioneering implementation of the **Gemini Nano** on-device model. As noted in the [developers.googleblog.com/gemini-nano](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemini-nano) technical overview, LAVA fundamentally shifts the architecture of AI interaction by keeping the entire inference process local to the user's hardware.

### Key Areas of Recognition:
1. **Architectural Innovation:** Integration of local LLMs without compromising stability (Reference: [chromium.org/design-docs](https://chromium.org/design-docs)).
2. **Privacy Leadership:** Establishing a "Zero-Server" standard for data sovereignty.
3. **Accessibility Breakthroughs:** Creating a seamless, hands-free voice interface.
4. **Resource Efficiency:** High token-per-second output with minimal battery drain.
